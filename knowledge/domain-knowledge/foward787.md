Forward (FWDPR) is a Puerto Rico–based business community and ecosystem founded in 2018 in the aftermath of Hurricane María, with the goal of revitalizing the island's economy. It brings together a portfolio of companies and brands — including RVPR, Nave, Xtillion, Platea, Expert Flyer, nBeta, Abarca, Ticketera, Findit, Amplia MLS, and the Criollos de Caguas — spanning industries like finance, data, marketing, real estate, entertainment, sports, and consumer marketplaces. These teams work collaboratively under the Forward umbrella, sharing a commitment to excellence and delivering value to consumers and partners. Forward's mission is to attract and nurture talent in Puerto Rico, position the island as a hub for innovation, and drive economic growth through community building and social impact.
