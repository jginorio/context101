Findit (finditpr.com) is a real estate marketplace hyper-focused on the Puerto Rico market. It displays for-sale, for-rent, and sold listings (sold is still in beta). At the moment, its strengths are on-market properties (for sale and for rent).

Findit was created to give locals a better experience when searching for properties. The majority of real estate agents in Puerto Rico are not Realtors, meaning they can't use the Multiple Listing Service (MLS). This is why platforms like Zillow and Realtor.com — which rely solely on MLS feeds — are not widely used or recognized locally. The language barrier adds to this — those platforms are only in English, while Findit offers a Spanish version by default along with an English version. Findit gave agents the ability to publish listings manually or through MLSs. Currently, Findit gets its listings from agents or For Sale by Owner (FSBO) sellers who input their properties manually, as well as from Stellar MLS and Amplia MLS. Findit does not charge for publishing listings, so anyone can publish. It also includes a professional profile for agents, giving them more exposure via SEO, a better way to showcase their listings, and a spot on Findit's directory page.

As of April 1, 2026, Findit has over 120K monthly sessions on its website, over 70K followers on Instagram (@findit.pr), over 24K likes on Facebook, and over 7K followers on TikTok (@finditpr.com) — though TikTok hasn't been actively used in some time.
